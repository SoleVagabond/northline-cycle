# Northline product comparison

Updated October 4, 2026. This compares implemented capabilities with previously reviewed official product documentation. Vendor accounts were not purchased or tested. Sources establish documented behavior, not comparative usability, reliability, satisfaction or hiring outcomes.

| Area                  | Documented commercial benchmark                                                                                                                                                            | Northline workshop release                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Estimates             | [RepairDesk](https://help.repairdesk.co/portal/en/kb/articles/how-can-i-create-and-manage-an-estimate) documents customer-portal approval and mandatory customer decisions.                | Multi-part quantities, server prices, version-specific approval, declines and alternatives are implemented. Separate authenticated customer approval remains absent.                                                                        |
| Inventory             | [Lightspeed](https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/229131428-Creating-and-completing-work-orders) documents parts reservations, waits and completion.                  | Receipts, availability after reservations, competing-repair protection, shortage waits, resumption, consumption and a ledger are implemented. Purchasing and configurable large inventories are absent.                                     |
| Scheduling            | [Citrus-Lime](https://howto.citruslime.com/creating-and-working-on-a-workshop-job/291794-scheduling-a-workshop-job) documents a calendar, unscheduled-job search and exception indicators. | Due dates, assignment, priorities, calendar navigation, unscheduled jobs, overdue filtering and daily capacity checks are implemented. Drag-and-drop, time slots and configurable staff calendars are absent.                               |

Reports distinguish payment from repair value; summaries print and records export. No charging, deposits, emailed quotes, tax invoices or refunds are processed. |
| Overview              | [Lightspeed Home](https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009800674-Understanding-Home) separates active, overdue and finished work.                                  | Dashboard, search, filters, workload, low-stock indicators, completed service value and outstanding balances are implemented. Multi-location analytics and unrestricted reports are absent.                                                 |

The previous review identified operational queues, scheduling and inventory as missing. This release implements them within one saved workflow from priced intake through collection and payment records. A protected Node installation also adds an operator key, expiring sessions, custom intake and documented backups. Public visitor mode remains fictional and openly switchable between workflow views.

Established systems have substantially broader commercial scope. Northline has twelve fixed services, three mechanics, six stocked parts, one operator key, whole USD prices, one timezone and explicit growth limits. It has no independently permissioned staff/customer identities, purchasing or external transactions/communications. Its source, recoverable failures and reproducible tests provide portfolio evidence; they do not establish equivalent overall product scope or predict employment.

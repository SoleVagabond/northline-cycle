# Northline product comparison

Updated October 5, 2026. This is a comparison against documented vendor capabilities, not hands-on testing of competitor accounts or a claim of equivalent commercial readiness.

| Area                      | Documented benchmark                                                                                                                                                                 | Northline work-order release                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Estimates                 | [RepairDesk](https://help.repairdesk.co/portal/en/kb/articles/how-can-i-create-and-manage-an-estimate) documents customer approval through its portal.                               | Multiple services and compatible parts share one version-specific decision; exact cent totals and historical snapshots persist. Revocable, expiring links let a separate recipient decide on one repair. Possession authorizes access; customer identity is not verified. |
| Inventory                 | [Lightspeed work orders](https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/229131428-Creating-and-completing-work-orders) documents reserved stock, waits and completion.    | Configurable models/SKUs, prices, reorder levels, receipts, reservations, shortage waits and consumption are implemented. Supplier purchasing, returns and automated compatibility checks remain absent.                                                                  |
| Scheduling                | [Citrus-Lime](https://howto.citruslime.com/creating-and-working-on-a-workshop-job/291794-scheduling-a-workshop-job) documents scheduling, unscheduled work and exception indicators. | Work and collection dates stay separate. Staff workdays, time off, shop opening days/hours and daily budgets are configurable. Existing conflicting plans are flagged; unknown budgets stay unknown. Drag-and-drop and timed appointment capacity remain absent.          |
| Customer records          | Northline's [bike-shop business review](BIKE-SHOP-REVIEW.md) connects intake, inspected costs and later collection.                                                                  | Customer/bike records retain repeat visits, intake condition, findings, customer updates and private/shared photos. Migration does not merge people by name. No customer account directory is exposed through repair links.                                               |
| Payment and communication | [Lightspeed work orders](https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/229131428-Creating-and-completing-work-orders) documents deposits and emailed quotes.             | Offline payments and collection are separate; summaries print and records export. Shared in-app updates and manually copied links are available. No charges, deposits, email delivery, tax invoices or refunds are processed.                                             |

Northline now implements a coherent bounded work-order workflow with repeat-bike histories, itemized approvals and configurable shop data. The public portfolio remains fictional; protected Node mode uses one operator key and separate customer capabilities. Staff permissions, supplier operations, accounting and multi-location administration remain commercial gaps. Passing tests and reproducible source provide engineering evidence; they do not establish a hiring outcome or parity with a mature retail platform.
